Velvet Nadir
Decentralized Ephemeral AI
The Problem

Security & Centralization

Modern AI systems funnel deeply personal context—biometrics, daily habits, and private conversations—into centralized corporate silos, creating massive honeypots and surrendering user sovereignty over their own data.

The Local-First Perimeter

Velvet Nadir establishes a rigid "Dual Perimeter." It enforces absolute privacy by ensuring raw conversational data, audio, and visual state never leave the local Zenoh mesh. Processing happens on your hardware, not in the cloud.

Context Fragmentation

We exist across multiple domains (Home, Workshop, Business) using disparate devices. Current systems require constant context-switching and manual synchronization, lacking an understanding of where we are and what matters right now.

Parallel Context Tracks

A unified context stream maintains parallel domain tracks. The system inherently understands your location and engagement level, seamlessly shifting focus (e.g., from a 'Workshop' CAD project to a 'Business' escalation) without explicit prompting.

Intrusive Interfaces

Computing has become overly complex, demanding our visual attention through screens and convoluted UIs. Technology should empower us, not distract us, yet we are constantly forced to adapt to the machine's interface.

Audio-First Ambience

UI is treated as a last resort. Voice is the primary, frictionless interface. When visual information is necessary, the agent generates ephemeral, semantic wireframes rendered contextually to the nearest appropriate display, allowing the technology to disappear into the background.

The Mission
Velvet Nadir is a truly personal AI assistant designed to run entirely on your own hardware. It perceives the world through distributed sensors, maintains persistent context across domains, acts proactively via graduated autonomy, and remains infinitely extensible.

Audio-First

Information is generated semantically and displayed contextually only when needed. Voice remains the primary interface.

Local-First

Process on-device. The system guarantees absolute privacy via a dual perimeter, keeping sensitive biometric data within the local mesh.

Context-Aware

The system tracks Personal, Workshop, and Business domains, dynamically assessing your engagement level to surface relevant tasks.

Extensible

Powered by the Zenoh fabric. Add new devices instantly. The Saraswati pipeline allows the system to observe, learn, and hot-load new skills.
