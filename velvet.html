Velvet Nadir
Decentralized Ephemeral AI
The Mission
Velvet Nadir is a truly personal AI assistant designed to run entirely on your own hardware, free from cloud dependencies. It perceives the world through distributed sensors, maintains persistent context across domains, and acts proactively on your behalf.

Audio-First

UI is a last resort. Voice is the primary interface. Information is generated semantically and displayed contextually only when needed.

Local-First

Process on-device. The system guarantees absolute privacy via a dual perimeter, keeping sensitive biometric and contextual data within the local mesh.

Context-Aware

The system knows WHERE you are, WHAT you're doing, and WHO you're with through parallel tracking of Personal, Workshop, and Business domains.

Extensible

Built on the Zenoh communication fabric. Add new devices instantly. The Saraswati pipeline allows the system to learn and hot-load new skills.

Mesh Communication
The device mesh is powered by Zenoh (Zero Overhead Network Protocol). It replaces traditional MQTT/gRPC setups, providing broker-free, peer-to-peer pub/sub, RPC, and streaming capabilities across nodes.
